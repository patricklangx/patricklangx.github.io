IOTA Rebased Charts

Mainnet · epoch data synced

This page displays charts of three metrics for the IOTA Rebased Network:

  • Burnt IOTA per Epoch - The tokens burnt due to gas fees paid for transactions and operations (permanently removed from the network)
  • Net Minted IOTA per Epoch - The difference between the newly minted (767k tokens per epoch) and burnt tokens (in-/deflation)
  • Number of Transactions per Epoch
An epoch lasts one day (from 9:39 AM to 9:39 AM CET the next day)
